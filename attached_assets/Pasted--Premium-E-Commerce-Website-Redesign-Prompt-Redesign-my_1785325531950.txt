# Premium E-Commerce Website Redesign Prompt

Redesign my existing e-commerce website from the ground up. The current functionality is good, but the overall design feels outdated and lacks the premium, modern experience I'm aiming for. Do **not** simply refresh the UI—rethink the entire layout, visual hierarchy, spacing, and user experience while keeping the existing functionality intact.

## Overall Vision

Create a website that combines:

* **Apple's premium, minimalist aesthetic** (clean layouts, generous white space, bold typography, elegant product presentation, subtle animations).
* **Amazon's powerful shopping experience** (fast product discovery, intuitive navigation, advanced search, clear categories, personalized recommendations, and frictionless purchasing).
* A **modern Ghana-first shopping experience** that feels trustworthy, fast, and professional while appealing to customers buying a wide range of products.

The final result should feel like a high-end retail brand rather than a generic marketplace.

---

# Design Principles

* Premium and elegant.
* Minimal but information-rich.
* Large spacing and excellent visual hierarchy.
* Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Fast and intuitive navigation.
* Accessibility-first.
* Modern micro-interactions.
* Smooth transitions and animations.
* Consistent design language across every page.

Avoid clutter, oversized borders, excessive shadows, or unnecessary gradients.

---

# Color Palette

Use a clean premium palette inspired by Apple.

Background:

* White (#FFFFFF)

Secondary Background:

* Light Gray (#F5F5F7)

Primary Text:

* Near Black (#1D1D1F)

Borders:

* Soft Gray

Accent Color:
Use my brand color sparingly for CTAs, links, highlights, and interactive states.

The interface should never feel overly colorful.

---

# Typography

Large modern headings.

Suggested hierarchy:

* Hero: 56–72px
* Section Titles: 36–48px
* Product Titles: 18–22px
* Body: 16px
* Captions: 13–14px

Use generous spacing between headings and content.
